Alchohalt — Legal & Privacy

Alchohalt is a private, on-device drink-tracking and mindfulness app. What you log stays on your phone by default — there is no account system and no server that stores your data. These documents describe exactly how the app handles your data, your rights, and the terms of premium subscriptions.

Last updated: 2026-09-17  ·  Current version: v1.0

  • Privacy Policy How your data is handled: on-device by default, payment processing via Apple/Google, RevenueCat validation, and the opt-in AI Insights feature (first insight free, unlimited with Premium).
  • Terms of Service The terms governing your use of the Alchohalt app: eligibility (17+), no medical advice, no-account data responsibilities, subscriptions, licenses, warranties, and dispute resolution.
  • Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (WA/NV/CO/CT) Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370, Colorado CPA, and Connecticut CTDPA compliance: categories of consumer health data, your rights (access, delete, revoke, appeal), and the AI Insights consent design.
  • End User License Agreement (EULA) The license that lets you use the app: permitted and prohibited uses, open-source components, termination, and Apple-specific terms.
  • Subscription Terms Premium pricing ($4.99/mo, $24.99/yr, $69 lifetime), free-vs-premium feature matrix, auto-renewal disclosure, trials, refunds, and grandfathering.
